Featured Reads

Cybersecurity

Closing Security Gaps Before They Become Incidents

Strong security begins with visibility. Organizations should understand exposed systems, account access, sensitive information and how alerts become an actionable response. Patching, least-privilege access, multi-factor authentication, tested backups and incident procedures form a practical foundation.

Crypto Investigations

Following the Blockchain

Blockchain transactions can provide a chronological trail of addresses, transfers and interactions. A responsible investigation separates what the blockchain demonstrates from conclusions that require independent evidence.

OSINT

From Public Data to Intelligence

Effective OSINT requires defining the question, collecting relevant public information, checking reliability, documenting provenance and avoiding unsupported conclusions. Good intelligence is transparent about uncertainty.

Compliance

Crypto Compliance Basics

Digital-asset businesses need processes that identify and manage financial-crime and operational risks. Depending on jurisdiction and business model, this can include due diligence, transaction monitoring, escalation and recordkeeping.

Identity Protection

First Steps After Identity Compromise

Secure affected accounts, change compromised credentials, enable MFA, notify relevant financial institutions and preserve records. Never provide passwords, private keys or recovery phrases to someone claiming they can recover funds.

Asset Tracing

Preserving Evidence After a Crypto Scam

Preserve transaction hashes, wallet addresses, platform names, relevant URLs, communications, payment records and a timeline. Be cautious of recovery services that guarantee results or request seed phrases or private keys.
